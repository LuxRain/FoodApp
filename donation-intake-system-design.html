Decision and record

Recommended first release

Barcode lookup, expiry OCR, human review, image evidence, and auditable inventory form the complete MVP.

Machines prepare the record. People approve safety-critical risk.
Always verify uncertain dates

Do not guess whether a printed value is an expiry date, manufacture date, or lot code.

Treat allergens as high risk

OCR and AI can propose allergens, but a volunteer must confirm them against package evidence.

Preserve conflicting evidence

Keep the barcode result, raw OCR text, photos, edits, and verifier identity together.

Support quarantine explicitly

Damaged packaging or uncertain cold-chain handling needs a reversible operational state.

Model the physical food, not just the product.

A product describes reusable facts. An inventory lot describes what arrived, where it is stored, and when it should move.
